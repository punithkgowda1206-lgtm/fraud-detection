How real-time AI stops loss before it posts

Card networks authorize in ~100ms. Sentinel sits in that window: it reads the live transaction plus the customer’s history, scores it, and either lets settlement continue or holds the funds. A hold means the merchant never receives the money, so a stolen-card purchase cannot become a loss.

  1. Transactional features. Amount, merchant, device, geo, channel, and time are joined to the customer baseline (typical spend, countries, devices).
  2. Signals, not a black box. Velocity, impossible travel, card testing (tiny charges then a spike), new device, and risky merchants each add an explainable weight.
  3. Decision policy. Score ≥ 72 blocks before settlement. 42–71 goes to an analyst queue. Below 42 auto-approves so good customers are not delayed.
  4. Human loop. Analysts confirm fraud or clear a false positive. That label can later retrain a production model.

This demo uses a transparent weighted model so you can see every reason. In production the same event stream would feed gradient-boosted trees or a sequence model, with rules as a safety layer.
